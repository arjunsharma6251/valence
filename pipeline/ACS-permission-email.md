# Draft: permission request to ACS (USNCO office)

To: olympiad@acs.org
Subject: Permission to reuse past USNCO exam questions in a free practice tool

Hello,

I'm Arjun Sharma, a two-time USNCO High Honors recipient and a Science Olympiad tournament director. I'm building Valence (usevalence.app), a free practice tool for students preparing for the USNCO Local Section and National exams. It is non-commercial, has no ads or payments, and is being reviewed with USNCO alumni and coaches.

I'd like written permission to reproduce questions from the publicly posted past exams (Local Section Exams and National Exam Parts I and II) inside the tool, with:

- attribution on every question ("USNCO 2024 Local Section Exam, Q17. © American Chemical Society") and a link to the original PDF on acs.org;
- a clear "not affiliated with or endorsed by ACS" notice;
- original explanations written by the Valence community (the official ACS solutions are used only as the answer key);
- immediate removal of any content on request.

The goal is the same as the existing community resources (topic-tagged practice with feedback), with the addition of adaptive selection and free-response feedback. I'm happy to follow any conditions ACS sets, including a review of the site before launch, a specific attribution format, or a limit on which years may be used.

Thank you for considering it, and for running the program.
