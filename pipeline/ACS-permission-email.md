I should have written before publishing rather than after, and I'm asking now for written permission to keep them up. If ACS would rather I didn't, tell me and I will remove them the same day.

What is there today:

- Questions from the posted Local Section and National Part I exams, and the National Part II free-response problems, re-typed with the chemistry rendered as text rather than scanned images.
- Attribution on every item, for example "USNCO 2024 National Part I exam, question 17. © American Chemical Society; reproduced for non-commercial practice", and a "not affiliated with ACS" notice in the footer.
- Explanations drafted by an AI model and labelled "draft" until a human verifies them. The official ACS solutions are used only as the answer key, not republished.
- Free-response answers scored against the official rubric by a model, shown as a study aid and not an official mark.

I am happy to work within whatever conditions ACS sets: a particular attribution format, a limit on which years may be used, review of the site before further promotion, removal of the free-response material, or anything else.
